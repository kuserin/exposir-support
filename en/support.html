Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Exposir need camera access?

Exposir uses your camera to measure the light in front of you in real time. The camera feed is never recorded or saved.

How do I lock the exposure?

Tap the lock button on the main screen. The EV value will be frozen so you can take your time composing your shot.

How does Long Exposure mode work?

Open Long Exposure mode from the main screen. Select your aperture, ISO, and ND filter, and Exposir will calculate the correct shutter speed and start a countdown timer.

Does Exposir work with film cameras?

Yes. Exposir is designed for any camera that requires manual exposure settings, including film cameras and cameras with manual lenses.

Is there a widget available?

Yes. You can add Exposir to your Home Screen or Lock Screen via the widget gallery.
