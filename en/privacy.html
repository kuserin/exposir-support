Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 8, 2026

Exposir (the "App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your data.

1. Information We Collect

The App may collect the following information:

  • Camera data: The App accesses your device camera solely to measure light levels. Images are never saved or transmitted.
  • Contact information: When you submit a support request, we collect your name, email address, and message content.
  • Crash reports: Anonymised crash reports may be collected to help us improve app stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

Collected information is used solely to:

  • Provide and improve the App's functionality
  • Respond to support enquiries
  • Fix bugs and improve quality

3. Sharing With Third Parties

We will not share your personal information with third parties without your consent, except as required by law.

4. Data Storage

Contact enquiry data is stored securely on Supabase servers (United States) using encryption. Please refer to Supabase's own Privacy Policy for details of their data handling practices.

5. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. Please contact us via the support form to exercise these rights.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the support page.
